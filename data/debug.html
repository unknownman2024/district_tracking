Shah Rukh Khan's Support Helps Deool Band 2 Hit Rs. 82 Crore; Film Nears All-Time Top 3 Marathi Record

BY SACNILK

Every once in a while, a regional film comes along that proves storytelling and audience connection can overcome even the biggest financial hurdles. The journey of the Marathi film Deool Band 2 is a perfect example of this. Made on a modest budget of approximately 8 to 10 Crore, the makers faced a major crisis regarding the Digital Cinema Package just before the release. This is the standard format required for theatrical screenings, and for a film of this scale, the costs involved were significant. Director Pravin Tarde recently shared that they had initially budgeted around 12 lakh for this digital processing. However, when the final estimate arrived, the bill had jumped to nearly 42 lakh. Being a regional film with limited resources, arranging an additional 30 lakh at the last minute was a nearly impossible task. Tarde mentioned that they had no immediate source to arrange such a sum. They eventually approached the team at Shah Rukh Khan's production house, Red Chillies Entertainment, to explain their situation. They highlighted that as a Marathi film with a tight budget, they needed support to ensure the movie could release on a large scale. In a generous move that prioritized art over commerce, the fee was waived, ensuring the film reached the audience on time. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) What makes this story truly special is Shah Rukh Khan’s personal perspective on the situation. When he learned about the struggle of the Deool Band 2 team, his reaction was rooted in his respect for the craft. He reportedly expressed that the film itself is far more important than the money involved, and that art should always take priority over commercial fees. By deciding to waive the entire 42 lakh bill, he sent a clear message that a good story deserves its place in the spotlight, regardless of the budget. This gesture ensured that the movie reached the audience exactly as planned, without any compromise. Today, as we look at the box office numbers 30 days after its release, that helping hand has resulted in one of the biggest success stories in Marathi cinema. Deool Band 2 has recorded a total India gross collection of 82.19 Cr. In terms of net collections, the film has earned a massive 70.22 Cr so far. What is quite interesting is that the entire collection has been generated solely from the domestic market, with overseas collections standing at zero. Playing across 40,884 shows in its first month, the film has maintained steady footfalls that have completely recovered its small budget many times over. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) The film is now standing on the doorstep of a historic milestone. It currently needs less than 1 Crore to surpass the India net collection of Raja Shivaji. If it manages to bridge this small gap, it will officially become one of the top three highest-grossing Marathi films of all time in terms of net earnings. This is an incredible achievement when you consider the scale of the two projects. Raja Shivaji was a massive historical epic with a high budget and the significant star power of Riteish Deshmukh. For a comparatively smaller production like Deool Band 2 to achieve similar numbers shows how deeply the content has resonated with the public. Reaching this level of success is a huge win for Pravin Tarde and the entire team. It proves that while big budgets and star names provide a great starting point, a film's long-term survival at the box office depends on its connection with the audience. With the momentum it still carries, the film is expected to cross the Top 3 mark very soon, cementing its place as a legendary blockbuster in the history of regional cinema.
