A massive part of this financial dominance in the modern era comes from the creation of the YRF Spy Universe. By bringing in a new-age directorial voice for their first-ever horror feature, the studio is looking to capture a segment of the audience that has shown a growing appetite for atmospheric and narrative-heavy thrillers. Ultimately, this pivot into horror is a bold but calculated step for Aditya Chopra. The studio has already mastered the art of the romantic drama and the blockbuster action film, and entering a new creative space is a way to test their boundaries. With pre-production already underway and a massive theatrical release planned for 2027, the industry will be watching closely to see how the first horror chapter in the YRF legacy begins to take shape. Given their history of redefining genres, there is a strong expectation that this venture will set a new benchmark for horror in mainstream Indian cinema.

The Multi-Billion Dollar Box Office: Inside The World's Top 5 Highest-Grossing Movie Franchises

BY SACNILK

Mumbai, August 7 - The global box office has witnessed several iconic film franchises entertaining audiences for decades, but only a handful have managed to generate tens of billions of dollars at the worldwide box office. From superhero epics and space operas to fantasy adventures and spy thrillers, these franchises have shaped modern cinema while consistently delivering blockbuster numbers at the box office. Here are the top 5 highest-grossing film franchises in the world. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) 1. The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) – $33.40+ Billion The Marvel Cinematic Universe stands alone at the top, boasting a cumulative box office total that nearly triples its closest competitor across 39 films so far. Initiated by Marvel Studios in 2008 with Iron Man, the MCU revolutionised Hollywood by pioneering the serialised, interconnected "shared universe" model on a massive scale. Spanning dozens of films across multiple storytelling "Phases," the franchise relies on cross-character momentum. Solo films build the cruiocity, which is then cashed in during massive crossover spectacles. The pinnacle of this strategy, 2019’s Avengers: Endgame, pulled in a staggering $2.79 billion, briefly becoming the highest-grossing film of all time. 2. Spider-Man – $12.20+ Billion While Spider-Man is a Marvel character, his film rights history makes this a distinct and phenomenally lucrative standalone franchise. This multi-generation tally includes the foundational 2000s Tobey Maguire trilogy, Andrew Garfield’s Amazing Spider-Man duology, Tom Holland's trilogy (co-produced by Sony and Marvel Studios), and the Oscar-winning animated Spider-Verse series. The web-slinger's enduring popularity was put on full display with 2021's Spider-Man: No Way Home, which generated $1.92 billion globally as the highest-grossing Spider-Man movie (so far), anchoring the web-slinger firmly in the number two spot. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) 3. Star Wars – $10.70+ Billion When George Lucas launched a space-fantasy epic in 1977, few could have predicted it would evolve into a cultural phenomenon. The Star Wars franchise is unique because its revenue is spread across generations, encompassing the Original Trilogy, the Prequels, the Disney-produced Sequel Trilogy, and standalone anthology pieces like Rogue One. The franchise's highest financial peak arrived in 2015 with Star Wars: The Force Awakens, which grossed $2.07 billion worldwide, proving the timeless, cross-generational pull of the Skywalker legacy. 4. The Wizarding World (Harry Potter) – $9.65+ Billion J.K. Rowling's literary phenomenon smoothly transitioned into a box office juggernaut starting in 2001. The core eight-film Harry Potter series captured the hearts of an entire generation, growing in tone, complexity, and financial power as its young actors grew up on screen. The franchise concluded its main arc flawlessly with 2011's Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2, which hauled in $1.34 billion. The universe has since expanded via the Fantastic Beasts prequel spin-offs, maintaining its rank as the premier fantasy franchise in film history. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) 5. James Bond – $7.80+ Billion The legendary British MI6 secret agent holds the ultimate crown for franchise endurance. Spanning over six decades and 25 official films produced by Eon Productions and Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer, 007 has continually reinvented himself to stay relevant across generations. From Sean Connery's suave beginnings in the 1960s to Roger Moore's playful 1970s blockbusters, James Bond laid the structural foundation for modern action cinema. The modern Daniel Craig era supercharged the franchise's financial footprint. This grittier, serialised reboot produced the franchise's biggest financial peak with 2012's Skyfall, which remains the only Bond film to breach the billion-dollar mark at $1.11 billion globally. (Data via Culture Crave/X)
