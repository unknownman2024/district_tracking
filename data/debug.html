Overseas, MIB has scored an estimated 16 crores, pushing worldwide gross to around 51.50 crore in 5 days. The movie had already built strong momentum over its opening weekend, thanks to positive audience response and solid overseas performance. The film opened with a worldwide gross of around 13 crore on its first day and maintained steady growth through the weekend. By Day 3, the Samantha-led entertainer had already crossed 40 crore globally, emerging as one of the strongest openings for a female-led Telugu film. With weekday collections holding reasonably steady, trade now expects Maa Inti Bangaaram to continue its successful theatrical run through the coming weekend.
