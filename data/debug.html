Cocktail 2 Box Office: Rom-Com Sequel Tops 100 Crore Worldwide Within First Week

As per estimates, the film crossed the 100 crore worldwide milestone by Day 6 of its theatrical run. In India, the sequel to 2012's movie has grossed approximately 79 crore (66.25 crore net) while overseas gross is 24 crore (estimated) so far, for the worldwide total of 103.25+ crores gross. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) Released on June 19, the film opened strongly with around 13.5 crore net in India on Day 1, making it one of Shahid Kapoor's biggest openings after Kabir Singh. By the end of the opening weekend, Cocktail 2 had already grossed over 76 crore worldwide, signalling audience interest in the romantic entertainer. It was, however, important for the movie to hold well during the weekdays to have the chance to emerge as a box office success, and it did well by keeping the momentum with regular drops. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) But things will now get tough for Cocktail 2 to continue the momentum with the arrival of Akshay Kumar's Welcome To The Jungle on June 26. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) Produced by Maddock Films and Luv Films, Cocktail 2 grossed an estimated 57 crores (47.50 crore net) over its 3-day opening weekend. The sequel showed healthy growth through Saturday and Sunday, indicating that audience reception is leaning towards the positive side. After a promising start on Friday, the film managed to build momentum over the weekend, which is always a crucial sign for urban multiplex-driven films. The weekend trajectory will come as a major relief for the makers, especially considering how difficult the theatrical market has become for Bollywood rom-coms in recent years. Positive word of mouth appears to be helping the film, with younger audiences responding well to the music, glamour, and relationship drama. The strong weekend growth suggests that the film has managed to click with its target audience, at least during the initial phase of its run. (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}) However, the real challenge for Cocktail 2 begins now as the film enters its crucial weekday period. The movie effectively has only one week of open run before Akshay Kumar's Welcome To The Jungle arrives in cinemas next week. A healthy trend through the weekdays could place the film in a comfortable position heading into its second weekend, but any major drop could quickly change the narrative. Both films are operating within the broader romantic drama space, and if the Imtiaz Ali directorial continues attracting family audiences in large numbers, it could impact the sequel's weekday and second-weekend business. For now, Cocktail 2 has done its job over the opening weekend with a solid 57 crore domestic gross and over 70 crores worldwide amid an encouraging audience response. But with competition intensifying and major releases arriving soon, the film now needs exceptional weekday stability to emerge as a clear box office success.
