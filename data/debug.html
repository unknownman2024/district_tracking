BY SACNILK

Mumbai, August 2 - The North American box office has achieved a historic milestone, delivering the biggest weekend of all time as Spider-Man: Brand New Day, The Odyssey, and a strong lineup of holdover releases combined to generate an extraordinary $434 million across all films during the July 31-August 2 weekend. The Marvel blockbuster also set a new all-time opening day record with $168 million on Friday, while Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey continued its phenomenal run by adding $51 million in its third weekend, becoming one of the strongest-holding event films in recent years.
